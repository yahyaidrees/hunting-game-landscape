## Rules taken from them
- **Light:** clear, bright morning sun from one side, crisp shadows, cool blue in shade, warm in sun.
- **Colour:** saturated but natural greens with yellow-green in sun and blue-green in shade; grey rocks with yellow-green moss and pale lichen; small spots of flower colour (yellow, white, pink, purple), never large areas.
- **Ground:** never one flat texture. Grass varies in tone and height; earth shows on paths and around rocks; mud where water collects.
- **Plants:** deodar pines as the main tree, oaks as the second; shrubs and tall grass hugging rocks; wildflowers in loose drifts.
- **Distance:** layered ridges fading to blue, snowy peaks on the skyline, mist in the low valleys.
- **Not wanted:** bare smooth hills, evenly spaced lone trees, one-colour grass, haze over the hunting area (it must read clearly through the scope).
